✅ Chama Best Practices (Kenya)
  • Registration: Register with CBoK or Ministry of Interior for legal recognition
  • Bylaws: Establish clear bylaws on contributions, withdrawals, and disputes
  • Record Keeping: Maintain accurate financial records and meeting minutes
  • SACCO Preference: SACCO accounts are safer and offer better rates than personal accounts
  • Transparency: Regular audits and member communication on investments
  • Dispute Resolution: Establish clear mediation process for conflicts
  • Merry-Go-Round Limits: Cap loan cycle to ensure all members get turn within reasonable time
  • Insurance: Consider group life insurance for member security
